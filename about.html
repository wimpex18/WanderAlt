About

We read the sources so you don't open nine tabs.

What this is

WanderAlt collects what's on in Tallinn's independent scene: DIY gigs, club nights, arthouse film, contemporary art and dance, talks and markets, and the shops and rooms that host them. Every listing carries a time, a walking distance and the source it came from. No hosts, no bookings, no commission. If a listing is wrong, a venue's own page is wrong, and we say where we read it.

The week as a calendar

Subscribe once and the week keeps itself current. Your calendar re-reads it twice a day; nothing is pushed and no account is involved.

What we store

Your saves, your interests and which listings you open, all in your own browser unless you sign in. Signed in, your saves, lists, follows, going marks and notification settings are kept in your account, with the notes in your inbox. Signing in with Google gives us your email address and nothing else. Alerts stay in the app: the inbox, and an optional push on a device you allow. We send no email announcements. No cookie banner, because there are no cookies to consent to. No analytics, no third-party scripts, no tracking between sessions. From You you can delete your account, which removes all of it, or forget everything on this device.

Terms, briefly

The listings belong to the venues and sources they came from; we link back to every one. There is no warranty that a door will be open when you arrive: we print what the source said and when we read it.
