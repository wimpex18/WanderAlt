WanderAlt About

We read the sources so you don't have to open nine tabs.

WanderAlt collects what's actually on in four Baltic and Nordic cities — gigs, club nights, screenings, markets, and the places that stay open late — and puts a time and a walking distance on every one of them. No hosts, no bookings, no commission. If a listing is wrong, it's because a venue's own page is wrong, and we'll say so.

What we store

Your saves, your city, and which kinds you open — all in your own browser. No cookie banner, because there are no cookies to consent to. No analytics, no third-party scripts, no tracking between sessions. You can wipe everything from You in one tap.

Terms, briefly

The listings belong to the venues and sources they came from; we link back to every one. Use the site as you like. It comes with no warranty that a door will be open when you arrive — we print what the source said and when we read it, and that is the whole promise.

The Saturday email

One email on Saturdays: what's on next week, in the same shape as Tonight. No account needed, one unsubscribe link, no other mail, ever. Sign up at the foot of Explore.

Or take it as a calendar

Subscribe once and the week keeps itself current. Your calendar re-reads it twice a day; nothing is pushed and no account is involved.
