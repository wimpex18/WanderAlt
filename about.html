About

We read the sources so you don't open nine tabs.

Independent culture in Tallinn. A time, a walk and a source on every listing.

What this is

DIY gigs, club nights, arthouse film, contemporary art and dance, talks and markets, and the shops and rooms that host them. No hosts, no bookings, no commission. If a listing is wrong, the venue's own page is wrong, and we say where we read it.

The week as a calendar

Subscribe once and the week stays current. Your calendar re-reads it twice a day. Nothing is pushed and no account is needed.

Privacy

Saves, interests and what you open stay in your browser. Signed in, your saves, lists, follows, going marks and alert settings also live in your account, with your inbox, until you delete it from You. Google sign-in gives us your email address and nothing else. Alerts stay in the app, and a push goes only to a device you allow. We send no email announcements. No cookies to consent to, no analytics, no third-party scripts.

Terms, briefly

Listings belong to the venues and sources they came from, and we link to each one. We print what the source said and when we read it. We cannot promise a door will be open.
