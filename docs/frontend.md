# Front end and design system

Plain HTML pages at the repo root, one `.js` renderer each, one stylesheet (`wa.css`). No framework and no build step. The direction, what users said and what is open are in `docs/design-brief.md`.

## Pages

|---|---|---|

Events has stable Today, Tomorrow and Weekend/This week shortcuts followed by Pick dates, kind facets and an image-first feed (five, then Show more, up to thirty). Shared: `i18n.js` and `lang/` (`WA.Lang`: the interface language, Languages below), `moods.js` (`WA.Moods`: taste), `discovery-state.js` (`WA.Discovery`: shared calendar intervals and filter predicates), `discovery-controls.js` (shared date/mood sheets and applied-filter keys), `tabbar.js` (the glass drop: the tab bar's, and `WA.glassDrop` for the language picker and the Map's Show control), `render.js` (`WA.R`: rows, cards, place rows, shelves, section heads, skeletons, empty states, areas, kinds, the "why" tag, interests, last visit), `icons.js` (`WA.Icon` UI icons, `WA.Picto` the Labels discs), `finder.js` (legacy finder helper retained on other pages; Now no longer loads it and uses one real field at every width), `ask.js` (`WA.Ask`: reads a search sentence into filters, in English, Estonian and Russian, places included: records, books, thrift, galleries, cinemas, clubs, bars, theatres, museums, craft beer), `route.js` (`WA.Route`: the evening). Data and state: `supabase.js`, `when.js`, `geo.js`, `hours.js`, `bookmark.js`, `lists.js`, `follow.js`, `report.js`, `seen.js`, `auth.js`, `theme.js`, `offline.js`, `install.js`, `sw.js`.

`supabase.js` loads `catalogue_redirects` alongside the catalogue. Retained event/venue ids resolve to canonical records in detail and map links, saves and list contents; raw saved ids remain stored so an admin undo is reversible. Saved looks up records absent from recommendations before declaring them gone, including unverified venues and archived events. Failed lookups retain the save and offer a retry; cached empty answers cannot establish absence. Detail offers the same recovery on lookup failure. Paged results are unique by id and use stable ordering. Tonight claims displayed ids across shelves, so tomorrow's cards do not repeat under Later this week. Separate dated performances keep their own ids.

## System

- **Surfaces.** White paper (`--paper`) over a soft daybreak gradient fixed behind the page (`--daybreak`, `body::before`: peach, lilac, apricot at low strength), a quiet fill (`--paper-2`), ink `#24222c` for type only. The dark theme (`data-theme="dusk"`) is warm charcoal, the way Claude's own dark theme reads: `#262624` paper, `#30302e` and `#3a3936` fills, cream type `#faf9f5`, a faint warm glow behind the page, the vermilion unchanged; no violet and no pure black. Never cream, and **no black surfaces**: selection is tinted glass with a vermilion ring (`--select-bg`, `--select-ring`), the one primary action is the vermilion gradient (`--primary-bg`, `--primary-glow`), and map clusters, toasts, offline bar and cancelled flags are tinted too. Text on a tint stays ink; white only on the gradient.
- **One accent**, Tallinn vermilion `--accent` `#d83a14`: the primary key (Tickets, Search), "now", the day-strip bars, the open ring on venue pins. White text on it is 4.7:1. Text in the accent uses `--accent-ink`.
- **Two quiet signals** beside it, each with one meaning and kept to a few places: green `--go` (`#19804f`, dusk `#5fd39a`) is open and free (open badges, the "3 open" count on Now's Places switch, "Free" in rows and posters, class `wa-free`); blue `--here` (`#2563d9`, dusk `#7ab4ff`) is you (the Near me switch, the walker icon beside walking times, the Guide's "Walking from" pin, your dot on the map). Everything else that is coloured stays vermilion; neither colour is used for decoration.
- **Liquid Glass is for what floats over content**, never for content: the top bar, the floating tab bar and its drop, map controls and the Show control, the finder sheet, every sheet's panel, the ticket bar, the toast, the route card (a card of soft gradient the glass bends). The card time badge is solid paper. One recipe (`--glass-*` tokens); `prefers-reduced-transparency` and browsers without `backdrop-filter` get solid paper.
- **Type.** Geologica (variable 300–800; Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic, self-hosted in `fonts/`), weights 500–700, tight tracking on headings. Geist Mono only for feed URLs.
- **Icons, two tiers.** UI icons: 24px, a 1.8 line in two tones, drawn the way a system symbol is drawn hierarchically: the line in `currentColor` and, where a shape has a body, that body (`class="t"`) filled at 16% (26% in dusk) behind it. Selected (the current tab, a pressed key, `.wa-ic--solid`) the body fills and the inner lines (`class="k"`) are cut out of it in `--ic-knock`. The static pages carry their icons inline with `data-ic="<name>"`; `npm run build:inline-icons` (`.scripts/sync-icons.js`) rewrites them from `icons.js`, and a test fails when they differ (`WA.Icon`). Labels: round, flat discs, one Tallinn object each on a muted ground with a faint groove ring like a record, for kinds and cities (`WA.Picto`, `.wa-picto--<name>`; the artwork is in `icons.js`, drawn on a 64 grid). They stand in for a picture when there is none and keep their own colours in both themes, because they paint pictures and the accent stays the interface's. The word beside a disc carries the meaning.
- **Shape.** Concentric radii: 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 28, pills for chips, search and pins. Cards 20px, sheets 28px.
- **Motion.** `--ease` and `--spring` only; press states scale, sheets rise with the spring. The route card on Now draws itself once, top down (the skyline inks in, the thread grows, nodes drop in, the listing's node pulses once; Another redraws it faster). `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all of it. Map sheet snaps set the final list height once and animate its position; category folding uses grid rows. Tab-pill and grip widths do not animate; the Map's Show pill widens to its option. The Now badge uses a static dot; shelves move only through user input. Temporary loading skeletons shimmer until data arrives. Impeccable exceptions retain the specified spring, Geist Mono feed URLs and the loading shimmer misidentified as a marquee.
- WCAG 2.2 AA; 44px tap floor; focus ring 2px ink.

## Patterns

- **Card**: square photo, glass badge for when ("Tonight · 19:00", "On now · till 23:00"), heart to save, title (two lines), venue · area, walk · price · why tag. No photo: the kind's Label centred on a quiet well.
- **Picture identity**: an event shows its own artwork; without it, its venue's logo or photo (marked "Venue logo" or "Venue photo" on the detail page); without either, its category Label. A venue's logo (`image_source: logo`) is drawn whole wherever a venue photo would sit: place rows, map cards, the venue link on an event, saved-list tiles, route stops, and, with a credit, the venue detail. Its tile follows the logo's measured tone (`image_tone`, from `pipeline/logo-tone.ts`, class `tone-*` from `WA.R.logoCls`): a logo on its own dark or coloured ground fills the box; on the light theme a light logo on a clear ground gets a dark tile; on the dark theme a dark one-colour logo (on a clear or white ground) is inverted onto the page colour, dark colourful ink is brightened, and a colourful logo printed on white sits on a dimmed tile. A logo with no tone keeps the plain tile. Photo retrieval and review rules are in `docs/data.md`.
- **Row** (Programme, venue programme, Saved): rail with time then walk (area when location is off), kind and why tag, English title, venue · area · price, thumbnail. A dated listing without a supplied time says "Time not listed", never claims to run all day.
- **Place row**: photo or Label, name, kind · area, open state (filled dot open, square shut, dashed not filed), walk.
- **Event page**: photo or Label, English title, 1–2 sentence English highlights, explicit performance languages when stated, facts as an icon list (When, Entry, Walk or Area), the ticket bar (price and the ticket seller's host, Tickets or Walk me there), Calendar / Walk there / Save / List, the venue card, a native Original description disclosure, Address (mini-map, address, Walking directions), then a compact source link, a quiet Share action and a collapsed Flag a problem disclosure. There is no attendance block or separate source section. On phones the tab bar steps aside and a back key appears.
- **Flags**: what the source says about a show, from `flag`. Cancelled and postponed are tinted pills with a strong edge; the card or row greys its photo, strikes the title, never reads "On now", and sorts last on Now's list; the event page opens with a tinted notice and drops tickets and calendar. Sold out and few tickets left are outlined pills; sold out turns Tickets into "Check for returns". Never the accent, which means now.
- **Saves and lists** (`save-store.js`, `bookmark.js`, `lists.js`): instant local edits with a persistent account-scoped outbox. Guest saves and lists move once to the account used to sign in. Sign-out shows a separate guest shortlist; another account never inherits the signed-out account's records or pending writes. Sync retries on sign-in, online, resume and edits; HTTP errors and timeouts retain the queue. A fresh pull replaces acknowledged data, so another device's removals apply; pending local edits take precedence. Concurrent edits to the same list use its last synced version. List writes include the account ID, and the own-row update policy allows renaming. Cross-tab account changes announce the new account and stale responses cannot overwrite it.
- **Original description**: closed by default. Opening it fetches at most 2,000 source characters, the original title and a link to the full source. Failed requests offer Try again and retain the source link. Known text languages use `lang`; performance languages come from explicit source statements, never from the announcement's language. English-source excerpts are labelled Source description. English highlights also feed page metadata and social previews.
- **Mini-map**: a still MapLibre map with the pin, in the page's theme; tapping it opens `map.html?pick=<id>`, which centres on that event or place and chooses it.
- **Venue page**: photo or a monogram of the name on its kind's tint, open state, Follow / Walk there / Save, labelled links (Website, Instagram, Facebook), a Next fact (the next show and how many follow), programme by day, the week's hours, Address with the mini-map, then a small OpenStreetMap attribution link when the venue has an OSM identity. There is no separate source section. Retained closed venues show a closure notice and never read Open now from old hours.
- **Follows** (`follow.js`) are keyed by identity: `place:<places.id>` for a venue, `src:<handle>` for a source. The stored value is the label You shows. Follows saved as bare names migrate to ids when the catalogue loads (a name shared by two places stays bare). A followed venue or source gets a Calendar link (webcal, the `calendar-feed` function with `place` or `handle`); the calendar app does the notifying, nothing is sent from the site. The source page has its own Follow button.
- **Saved searches**: when Programme has a kind, Free or In English set, a line under the count offers Follow this search and, once followed, Add to calendar. It is a `search:` follow (see `docs/data.md`) and appears on You.
- **Inbox** (`inbox.js`, You, signed in only): the notes the alert job wrote for the account, newest first, unread ones with an accent dot; opening You marks them read and clears the dot on the You button (`data-unread`). Clear inbox deletes them. Signed out, the section says what signing in gives.
- **Notifications** (`push.js`, You, section `#notifications`): optional. The section opens with a state line (Off, On for this device, Blocked, or Needs the Home Screen app), then "Notifications on this device" and "Tonight at places you follow", and when on, "Send a test notification" (made on the device, nothing from a server). Asking for permission happens on the tap. Hidden until the VAPID key is set. On an iPhone in a tab the section offers Add to Home Screen, which opens the install sheet; Blocked inside the installed app points to Settings > Notifications. `sw.js` shows the notification, opens a page on this site, and sets the Home Screen badge to the notifications still showing; `inbox.js` sets it to the unread count whenever a page opens or the count changes (Badging API, installed apps only).
- **Home Screen invitation** (`install.js`, `WA.Install`): iPhone and iPad only send web notifications from an installed app, and few know Share > Add to Home Screen exists. Three parts. The nudge is a glass capsule above the tab bar ("Make it an app"), shown **once, ever** (`shown` in `wa:install:v1`), on Tonight, Programme or Saved, after about 75 seconds of time spent looking at the app (counted across pages in the session), at a calm moment (no sheet, toast or focused field); dismissing it does not bring it back. A quiet entry stays for anyone who wants it: a text-weight "Add to Home Screen" line under the Tonight actions and a slim row near the top of You (`WA.Install.canOffer()`; hidden once installed, in in-app browsers, and where the browser cannot install). The sheet has three animated steps and a tile that pops into a grid, with platform wording: iPhone Safari (menu beside the address bar, then Share), iPad (Share at the top), Samsung Internet (menu, Add page to, Home screen) and other Android browsers (menu, Install app); Chrome and Edge use their own prompt, which Samsung 27+ does not fire. It warns a signed-out reader with saves that the installed iPhone app starts empty (iOS keeps its storage apart from Safari), and says "open in your browser" inside in-app browsers. The benefit line is one sentence per platform (`benefit()`), not a feature list. Inside the installed app, a signed-in reader who has not allowed notifications gets one invitation ("Alerts when plans change") that opens You at `#notifications`. The manifest carries `id`, `scope`, `categories` and three shortcuts; every page sets `apple-mobile-web-app-title`.
- **Deploys showing** (`sw.js`): Pages serves CSS and JS with `max-age=14400`, so a new service worker stores its shell with `cache: 'reload'` and refreshes a static file in the background with `cache: 'no-cache'` (a 304 when unchanged). A deploy shows on the next reload or the one after, not four hours later. Bump `VERSION` with every change to a shell file.
- **Staying fresh** (`offline.js`): an online catalogue outage or a service-worker fallback shows a quiet notice: last loaded listings stay visible with their actual cache age, or a failed first load says it cannot load listings. Try again retries the catalogue without navigation, preserving filters and saves; reconnect retries too. Only complete live catalogue reads clear the notice and allow absence detection. A retry shares one in-flight request. A page reloads itself when the Tallinn day has turned since it loaded, or when it comes back after more than 30 minutes out of sight (tab switch, app resume, back-forward restore), but never while a sheet is open or a field has focus. It also asks the service worker to check for a new deploy each time. Sessions are not time-boxed: the refresh token keeps a reader signed in until they sign out (Supabase's session time-box and inactivity limits are paid-plan settings and are off).
- **Sign-in** (`auth.js`): one sheet for new and returning readers, "Sign in or create an account": Google, or an emailed sign-in link (`/auth/v1/otp`, which creates the account on first use). The email link returns to the page with the session in the hash, as before. A reader whose email also carries a code (the Supabase Magic Link template must include `{{ .Token }}`; the default one does not) can tap "The email has a code" and type it (`/auth/v1/verify`). Email and password stays one step away ("Use a password instead", with Forgot password and Create account). Sessions last an hour; the refresh token is stored and renews them before they lapse, a second tab's sign-in or sign-out is picked up, sign-out revokes on the server, and a refused sign-in is shown on You. Sign-out first drops this device's push subscription, so a shared device does not keep the last reader's alerts. You also offers "Delete account" (calls the `delete-account` function, then wipes the device) and "Forget everything on this device" (every `wa:` and `wanderalt:` key plus the service worker's caches). There is no sign-in button in the page header; sign-in lives on You, and a password-recovery link opens the new-password sheet on whatever page it lands on. There is no phone sign-in (Supabase needs a paid SMS provider) and no Sign in with Apple (it needs a paid developer account); both break the free-only rule.
- Follows also sync to the account (`follows`), like saves. The alert job and the inbox are described in `docs/data.md`; email is dormant and has no switches on You.
- **Flag a problem** (`report.js`): a collapsed disclosure beside the compact source link in the event footer. One of five fixed reasons and an optional note of at most 280 characters, inserted into `problem_reports` (insert-only). `/review` lists the open ones with Fixed and Dismiss.
- **Measure from** (Programme filters): a place we hold, picked from a list, saved as `wa:anchor:v1`. It stands in for the device position in `WA.Geo` (`anchor`, `setAnchor`), so walking times and the distance slider work with no location prompt. Clearing the box returns to the device position.
- **Tab bar** (phones): four tabs: Now (a clock with the night spark; the first tab, so it reads right at noon as at midnight), Map, Saved, You, in a capsule sized to them (at most 348px, centred) whose columns follow the number of tabs, so no slot is ever empty. A vermilion-tinted pill and the icon drawn solid mark the current tab; the Programme and the Guide are not tabs (Now's Events and Places, search and filters lead there, and Now stays marked on them). The Guide stopped being a tab on 4 October: its list and Now's Worth the walk were the same picked places, and Worth the walk sat under the whole day's list; Places on Now replaces both. You is the fourth tab; the header holds search, language and theme. (`WA.glassDrop`, in `tabbar.js`, is the one implementation; the Map's Show control and the language picker use it too.) Press the bar (hold or start sliding) and it swells slightly while a drop of clear glass, taller and wider than a tab, lifts under the finger. The drop carries a magnified vermilion copy of the tabs clipped to its shape, so a tab half under it is half tinted. Letting go settles the drop on the nearest tab and opens it; a quick tap just follows the link. Pointer capture stays on the pressed element throughout the gesture; captured events bubble to the bar. Changing capture to the bar during lift fires a bubbling `lostpointercapture` and cancels the drag. Test with touch events and several moves, including a press on the SVG icon: mouse-only checks and capture-free mocks miss this failure. This applies to all three shared controls. A cancelled touch returns to rest without suppressing the next tap; released slides still suppress their follow-up click.
- **Search field** (Now, phones): a real `type=search` input in the pill, with a vermilion submit key. Enter sends the words to `discover.html?q=`, where the ask field reads a sentence as well as a title; empty goes to the Programme with its field focused. From 1024 the pill is Where / When / What slots that open the finder sheet.
- **Starting point** (Now, `start-from.js`, shared with You): the route card has one quiet From row between its title and stops. Its place name opens a small sheet with local venue/city suggestions; the adjacent 44px location key requests the device position directly. The primary Walk it and secondary Another remain together below the stops. No separate Near me control above the card. Selecting a point applies immediately, closes the sheet and recalculates the walk; incomplete typing or Cancel changes nothing. Suggestions begin at the first letter, use known coordinates, ignore closed places, rank prefixes first, and show at most eight results. Keyboard arrows/Enter, Escape, touch and mouse use the same combobox. The nearby mode is kept in `wa:near:v1`: a confirmed venue or device position turns it on; Whole city in the picker turns it off while retaining the origin for walking distances. Nearby routes start within 15 minutes and come in five-minute walking steps; event and place lists sort nearest first. No fresh location permission prompt at load. A failed location request keeps the selected venue and offers retry or browser-settings guidance. A selected venue is labelled "from here", never "from you". The origin control remains available when no route fits.
- **City and origin** (`city.js`, `geo.js`): city chooses the catalogue; starting point chooses coordinates. Only live cities enter autocomplete and automatic location matching (nearest live centre within 25km, no reverse-geocoding request). A successful device location on Now selects another covered city and reloads its catalogue; a manually selected starting venue stays authoritative. Tallinn is the sole live city. City entries carry a display name, aliases, centre and planned interface languages; Helsinki/fi, Riga/lv and Vilnius/lt/pl must get sources and translations before activation. Being in Riga today does not imply that we have Riga data. City matching never changes the interface language.

- **Rows that scroll sideways** (the chip rows on Programme, the Guide and Now's kinds; a row drawn after load calls `WA.UI.edges()`): the edge with more behind it fades out over 44px (30px on the kind row, whose half-visible tile must stay legible), and the fade follows the scroll (right at the start, both edges in the middle, left at the end; none when the row fits). `ui-helpers.js` sets `is-more-start` and `is-more-end` from the scroll position and size; `wa.css` draws the fade with a mask on `--fade-l` and `--fade-r`. A cut-off item reads as "more this way" without an arrow or a gradient overlay, so it works on any background, in both themes.
- **Light / Dark button** (top bar, every page): a bare 42px line icon with no disc, showing the mode a tap switches to (the sun while dark, the moon while light). One tap sets an explicit Light or Dark (`WA.Theme.set`, built in `theme.js` so no page repeats the markup); Auto remains the default until an explicit mode is chosen. Its label names the same mode.
- **Mood/price keys and sheets** (`moods.js`, `discovery-controls.js`): identical on Now and Map, with selected values visible outside the panel. Six moods can be chosen together and narrowed; ticket caps are Free, Up to €20, Any, with an explicit note that unknown prices stay in. Discovery offers all qualifying moods regardless of the current hour, because a future date can be selected. Routes still use current-hour availability. Draft changes apply with Show results; Escape, Close, backdrop and drag discard them. On Now, apply focuses the selected Events/Places segment and brings it into view above fixed navigation; cancel returns to the opener.
- **Kinds on Now** (`home.js`): under the day tabs, one scrolling row of the kinds in that day's list with their Labels and counts, most first (Gigs, Club nights, Film, Stage, Comedy, Art, Workshops, Talks, Festivals, Markets; Comedy read from tags), shown when the list holds two kinds or more. A chip narrows the list; tapped again it clears. The list is a preview: the next five in tighter rows (60 px picture, titles to two lines), then "Show N more" opens the rest in place (up to thirty) beside "All N", the Programme link, which carries the day, the kind and Near me. With a kind chosen, all of that kind shows.
- **Sheets follow the finger** (`ui-helpers.js`): on a phone every `.wa-sheet` drags down from its grip and head, and from its body when the body is scrolled to the top; let go past a third of the panel (at most 160 px) or with a flick and it closes, otherwise it springs back; upwards it gives a little. Closing goes through a cancelable `cancel` event, as Escape does, so a confirm hears it as Cancel. A tap on the backdrop closes any sheet.
- **Map**: the existing glass Show segment (All/Events/Places), a labelled date action, and the same mood/price and Near keys as Now. Counts and pins use the same date and ticket predicates as Now; Map excludes entries without coordinates. Its list remains compact to leave room for the map. Place inclusion still uses open-now or a matching event host; the legend states that place hours refer to now even when future events are selected. A direct `?pick=` link retains its focused entry regardless of filters.
- **Map style**: `map-style.json` (day) and `map-style-dusk.json` (night), warm charcoal like the dark theme, are generated by `npm run build:map-styles` from one layer list and two palettes in `.scripts/build-map-styles.js`; edit the palette there, not the JSON. Tiles and fonts are OpenFreeMap. Colour carries the city (green parks, blue water, warm main roads, dashed footpaths); there are no POI icons, because our pins are the points of interest.
- **Evenings in search** (`programme.js`, `route.js`): a plan in plain words ("plan my Friday evening", "what to do tonight", "план на вечер", "õhtu plaan"; `WA.Ask.places(q).plan`) puts an Evenings block above the listings: the stored routes that still hold for the day named (today when none), else one worked out in the page; the cards are the route cards from Tonight. The model, when it is asked, may also say `intent: evening`, `placeKinds` and `openNow`; its place kinds add the Places block (open ones only when asked) and an intent of places narrows the listings to those places. The listings below are always our own.
- **Places in search** (`programme.js`, `ask.js` `WA.Ask.places`): a query that names a kind of place ("records", "bookshop", "plaadipood", "пластинки") puts a Places block of up to four rows, picked ones first, above the listings; a place's name matches too ("terminal"). A shop word with no event or day in it ("vinyl shop", "second-hand") also narrows the listings to events at those places instead of reading as the Market kind. A day or an event word ("record store tonight", "vinyl fair") is a question about listings and shows no block. “Open now” (also in Estonian and Russian) keeps only places whose filed hours say they are open; unknown hours do not count. A places-only result hides the listing chips and the empty listings message; no matching places offers Clear search and the Guide. The place row carries a Picked tag and the pick note.
- **The next few hours** (`route.js`, `route-page.js`, card on Now): `plan({ mood, cap })` returns at most eight routes, best first, in this order: what starts within about two and a half hours (a stored evening that still holds, or one worked out here), then **places on foot** (two or three picked places, each at most ten minutes' walk from the one before, never the same kind twice running, open when you would arrive, daytime kinds before five), then later evenings. An evening is one listing with a start time that has not begun as the anchor; a **picked** place within 15 minutes' walk before it (record shop, bookshop, gallery, thrift, arts centre, cinema) and after it a picked bar, taproom or club within 12 (a bar after four, a club after nine) or, before five, another daytime place. A place before the listing must leave at least half an hour there after the walk. A place whose filed hours say it is shut when you would arrive is left out. A place with no filed hours is tried only inside the hours its kind usually keeps (`USUAL` in `route.js`: shops about 10 to 19, galleries and museums 11 to 18, bars 17 to 01, taprooms 15 to 23, clubs 23 to 04) and still says "hours not filed"; a cinema or theatre with no filed hours is no stop on its own. Places on foot use daytime kinds from 06:00 to 17:00, add bars, taprooms and clubs after that, and try nothing between 03:00 and 06:00, so a walk at midnight never sends anyone to a record shop. A stored route with an unfiled stop outside those hours is not shown. The listing's own venue is never its own before or after, and the places before and after it are distinct. Scores prefer a nearer start, a picked host, a matching interest, a matching mood and, when location is on, closeness to you. Times are worked out here from `now`, the walk (the site's one pace) and the listing's stated or assumed length. Nothing is invented. The pipeline also composes evenings for the next three days with a free model and stores them in `routes` (`docs/models.md`); the page reads them and checks every stop again. A route is its stops, so it lives in the URL (`route.html?s=place:<id>:<minute>,event:<id>:<minute>,…&d=YYYY-MM-DD`, validated on read with the Tallinn date and hours at arrival; expired dates, missing stops, changed event dates, and closed stops offer a fresh walk; `&t=<routeId>` carries a stored route's title and note for that same date). Its cost is the cheapest price of each listing ("tickets from €9", "price not listed", "free"). The card on Now is glass over a soft gradient: title, a line (area, length, cost), the stops with their walks, a hand-drawn skyline, Walk it (opens the route page) and Another (the next route, drawn again). The same card, without the buttons, lists More routes on the route page. `nextFrom(entry)` and `fromHere(entry)` serve After this (below).

## Speed

- **Snapshot.** `supabase.js` keeps the last catalogue in `localStorage` (`wa:catalogue:v2`, with a fingerprint of its text, and `wa:catalogue:at`, the time it was last confirmed; trusted for six hours, the pipeline's cadence, and never when that time is ahead of the clock) and draws it at once on a return visit, with `DATA_LIVE` false. Successful fresh responses confirm it; service-worker fallback responses retain their original cache timestamp and never renew the confirmation or become live. Actual event dates in Tallinn take precedence over snapshot labels such as Tonight.
- **Service worker.** Only exact public catalogue paths on the configured backend with the shipped public anon credentials enter the data cache, including catalogue redirects. Signed-in or secret-key requests bypass it. Only successful responses replace cache entries; an upstream HTTP error or network outage retains the last good catalogue. Static revalidation keeps failed responses out of the shell cache.
- **Intl formatters** in `when.js`, `geo.js`, `hours.js` and `render.js` are built once and their answers remembered. Building one per call cost about a second per filter tap on a few hundred listings; keep it that way.
- **Programme** answers a tap in the same frame with the chips, count and summary; the list and panel follow in the next task and repeated taps are drawn once. Markup is written only when it changed (`put`), so an unchanged list keeps its pictures.
- **CSS.** Rows below the fold use `content-visibility: auto`. Cards and day headings carry no live backdrop blur (dozens per screen); glass stays on the few things that float. `touch-action: manipulation` on controls.
- **Map.** MapLibre and the map start with the page, in parallel with the catalogue; the first fit waits for both. Pins are redrawn at most once a frame while the map moves.
- **Navigation.** `speculation.json` (sent as the `Speculation-Rules` header) prefetches the tab bar pages and any same-origin link on hover or press, and prerenders event pages on hover or press. Pages must be safe to prerender: no side effects at load, which is why the last-visit stamp is Tonight only, `detail.js` marks an event seen on `prerenderingchange`, and `brand-reveal.js` stands down while `document.prerendering`. `_headers` sends Early Hints for the home page (stylesheet, font, Supabase connection). Pages preconnect to Supabase; the map also to the tile host.

- **Map loading** (`maplibre-loader.js`, `sw.js`, `offline.js`): MapLibre GL JS 6.11.2 (the current release) and OpenFreeMap (`tiles.openfreemap.org/planet`) are kept. The service worker caches the `.mjs` bundles in `vendor/` like other static files (stale-while-revalidate; they were uncached, and Pages serves them `max-age=0, must-revalidate`, so every Map open paid revalidation round trips on a phone). They are not precached, so first load stays light: the first Map visit fills the cache, and on every other page `offline.js` fetches them once at idle, 3 s after load, unless Data Saver or a 3G-or-slower connection says no. `map.html` loads `maplibre-loader.js` first among the deferred scripts so the bundle import starts without waiting for the other 25, and preloads the OpenFreeMap TileJSON, the slowest serial hop. To confirm the cache on an iPhone: visit Tonight, wait 15 s, go offline, open the Map; the page, bundles and style load (tiles need the network).
- **Tab bar diagnostics** (`tabbar.js`): the bar has been seen floating mid-screen during momentum scrolling on an iPhone Home Screen app, intermittently, and has not been reproduced in the iOS 27 simulator. On phones it is given its own compositing layer. Holding the wordmark for 1.5 s switches on a readout (top right, kept per device in `wa:hud`) of the inner and visual viewport heights, the safe-area inset, and `off`, how far the bar's bottom is from where the layout says it should be (`worst` keeps the largest). Red means more than 2 px. A recording of a bad moment with `off 0` means the layout is right and only the drawing is wrong; a large `off` means the viewport is.
- **Sheets and the keyboard** (`ui-helpers.js`, `wa.css`): on iPhone the on-screen keyboard shrinks the visual viewport but not the layout viewport, so a bottom sheet used to sit under the keys with its password field and button hidden. `ui-helpers.js` publishes the visual viewport as `--vv-top` and `--vv-h` while the keyboard is up; `.wa-sheet` sizes to them and a focused field is scrolled into view. Return on the sign-in email field moves to the password.
- **You** (`you.js`, `start-from.js`, `wa.css`): signed out it opens on "Keep what you find", with Google and Email me a link (compact, side by side from 600px, stacked on phones); signed in, on an initial and email. Then Your taste (up to three kinds), the same Start from picker as Now, Inbox and Notifications when signed in, Add to Home Screen when offered, Following and Opened earlier as folds, Account when signed in, and Data and privacy as a fold. Language and appearance use the header only. A named origin is saved as `wa:anchor:v1`; clearing the field returns to the device position. Choosing Tallinn explicitly uses its city centre, with a note recommending a venue for precision. A known device position near Tallinn fills the field with Tallinn, but walking calculations still use the exact position. Unmatched positions leave City or place as the placeholder. Drafts and focus survive catalogue and language redraws. `geo.js` shares a requested position across navigation in the same tab through `sessionStorage` (`wa:position:v1`, accepted for five minutes, rejected when stale or in the future). It never enters localStorage, the catalogue or the backend. The location key refreshes the device position and clears the anchor only on success. A cancelled request cannot overwrite a venue chosen meanwhile. Sheets use a quiet Cancel; list-name sheets offer `WA.Lists.suggestions()` and create on Return.

Checked on Now and You in Chrome at 390, 744 and 1440px in both themes, with no horizontal overflow and 44px location targets. Checked keyboard selection, a real touch tap, partial-draft cancellation, language-change draft preservation, denied and successful simulated location, tab navigation, Whole city and late location replies after venue selection. Physical iOS Safari and its on-screen keyboard still need a post-deployment check.

The starting-point design follows [NN/g progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/) (show the current choice where it matters; reveal editing on request), [Apple location privacy guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy/) (request permission when a feature is used) and the [W3C combobox pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/combobox/) (suggestions, keyboard selection and focus). These support the design rationale; they do not substitute for usability testing.

- **Light motion** (`wa.css`, `view-transition.js`): compositor-only (transform and opacity), no new request, about 0.7 KB gzipped of CSS and one tiny script. The first five rows and four cards of a screen settle in over 260 ms with a 30 ms stagger, starting at 72% opacity so nothing is ever hidden and loading is not held back; this runs only while `html[data-enter]` is set (page start until 1.2 s after the first listings, or 5 s), so a refresh or a filter does not replay it. Rows, cards and places sink a hair under the finger; the Open-now dot breathes three times and rests; section headings ease up as they scroll into view where `animation-timeline: view()` exists (Safari 26+). Everything is off for reduced motion, and the entrance for Data Saver.
- **Runs that began earlier** (`render.js`, `runningSpan`): a listing with a start and an end where the start is before today and the end has not passed (an exhibition, a festival pass) is filed under today in grouped lists, not under the day it started, and its rail reads "29 Sep to 3 Oct" instead of a time. Before this, such listings appeared under a heading in the past.
- **Date-only runs** (`when.js`, `endsAtMs`): an end stored as local midnight with no stated start time means through that whole day (sources write "29 Sep to 3 Oct" inclusively), so the run ends a day later than the stored instant. Without this, a running exhibition vanished on the morning of its last day.
- **About** (`about.html`, `about.js`): separate from You on purpose. You is personal and local; About is the public page that people and the Google consent screen link to (`/about`), so it keeps stable anchors: `#what`, `#calendar-feed`, `#privacy`, `#terms`. It is a headline, a one-line lede, three counts from the live catalogue and four folds (`<details>`, What this is open); a link to an anchor opens its fold (`about.js`). The long privacy note lives only here; You's Data and privacy fold is two lines and links to it.

## Apple Maps and mini apps

Apple Maps can show opening hours in its own place cards, but the documented Server API and MapKit JS place objects expose no structured opening-hours field for the venue pipeline. Apple’s terms restrict extraction into a secondary database, permanent storage and displaying its map data without the corresponding Apple map. Keep MapLibre/OpenFreeMap and collect venue facts from our existing sources. MapKit JS can run on the website; it does not require a native app. Its standard allowance is attached to Apple Developer Program membership (USD 99/year, regional pricing), available to individuals without a registered company. See [Apple Maps](https://developer.apple.com/maps/web/), [Place](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/applemapsserverapi/place), [membership](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) and [Attachment 6](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/).

The [Mini Apps Partner Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/mini-apps-partner/) is for native iOS/iPadOS App Store host apps offering other developers’ mini apps. It requires Developer Program membership, an approved mini-app manifest, Advanced Commerce API, Declared Age Range API, In-App Purchase and refund consumption reporting. Its benefit is 85% of qualifying digital in-app purchase sales. WanderAlt’s standalone website and outbound event-ticket links do not match this program; it provides no free Maps entitlement or opening-hours feed.
