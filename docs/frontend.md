# Front end and design system

Plain HTML pages at the repo root, one `.js` renderer each, one stylesheet (`wa.css`). No framework and no build step. The direction is recorded in `docs/design-brief.md` under Decisions.

## Pages

| Page | Script | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `index.html` | `home.js` | Tonight: search field, category bar, the Near me and Pick your kinds keys, card shelves (on now, starting soon or late, for you, this weekend, later this week) and the interests sheet; side column with places open now, areas and the map card. |
| `discover.html` | `programme.js` | Programme: search, seven-day strip, kind chips, filters (sheet on phones, sidebar from 1024), list grouped by day. |
| `map.html` | `map.js` | Map: event pills and venue pins, a swipeable row of cards for the chosen pin, a sheet (sidebar from 1024) of what is in view by walking time. |
| `places.html` | `places.js` | Places by kind, nearest then open first, with an Open now toggle. |
| `detail.html` | `detail.js` | The event page and the venue page (one template, two shapes). |
| `saved.html` | `saved-page.js` | Lists, then coming up, places, and what is over. |
| `profile.html` | `you.js` | You: interests, appearance, follows, opened earlier, account. |
| `review.html` | `review.js` | Review queue for held-back events; unlinked, `noindex`. See `docs/data.md`. |
| `source.html`, `about.html`, `404.html` | `source.js`, `about.js`, `notfound.js` | A source's feed, About, not found. |

Shared: `tabbar.js` (the glass drop: the tab bar's, and `WA.glassDrop` for the Map's Show control), `render.js` (`WA.R`: rows, cards, place rows, shelves, section heads, skeletons, empty states, areas, kinds, the "why" tag, interests, last visit), `icons.js` (`WA.Icon` UI icons, `WA.Picto` pictograms), `finder.js` (the Where / When / What sheet from the desktop search pill; on phones the pill is a real field that sends its text to the Programme), `ask.js` (`WA.Ask`: reads a search sentence into filters). Data and state: `supabase.js`, `when.js`, `geo.js`, `hours.js`, `bookmark.js`, `lists.js`, `follow.js`, `report.js`, `seen.js`, `auth.js`, `theme.js`, `offline.js`, `sw.js`.

`supabase.js` loads `catalogue_redirects` alongside the catalogue. Retained event/venue ids resolve to canonical records in detail and map links, saves and list contents; raw saved ids remain stored so an admin undo is reversible. Saved looks up records absent from recommendations before declaring them gone, including unverified venues and archived events. Failed lookups retain the save and offer a retry; cached empty answers cannot establish absence. Detail offers the same recovery on lookup failure. Paged results are unique by id and use stable ordering. Tonight claims displayed ids across shelves, so tomorrow's cards do not repeat under Later this week. Separate dated performances keep their own ids.

## System

- **Surfaces.** White paper (`--paper`), a quiet fill (`--paper-2`), ink `#1c1c1e`. Dark theme (`data-theme="dusk"`) is `#111113` paper. Never cream.
- **One accent**, Tallinn vermilion `--accent` `#d83a14`: the primary key (Tickets, Search), "now", the day-strip bars, the open ring on venue pins. White text on it is 4.7:1. Text in the accent uses `--accent-ink`.
- **Liquid Glass is for what floats over content**, never for content: the top bar, the floating tab bar and its drop, map controls and the Show control, the finder sheet, the ticket bar, the card time badge, the toast. One recipe (`--glass-*` tokens); `prefers-reduced-transparency` and browsers without `backdrop-filter` get solid paper.
- **Type.** Geologica (variable 300–800; Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic, self-hosted in `fonts/`), weights 500–700, tight tracking on headings. Geist Mono only for feed URLs.
- **Icons, two tiers.** UI icons: 24px line, 1.8 stroke (`WA.Icon`). Pictograms: 48px line drawings for kinds and cities on a tinted tile in their own hue (`WA.Picto`, `.wa-picto--<name>`). Hue decorates; the word beside it carries the meaning.
- **Shape.** Concentric radii: 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 28, pills for chips, search and pins. Cards 20px, sheets 28px.
- **Motion.** `--ease` and `--spring` only; press states scale, sheets rise with the spring. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all of it. Map sheet snaps set the final list height once and animate its position; category folding uses grid rows. Tab-pill and grip widths do not animate; the Map's Show pill widens to its option. The Now badge uses a static dot; shelves move only through user input. Temporary loading skeletons shimmer until data arrives. Impeccable exceptions retain the specified spring, Geist Mono feed URLs and the loading shimmer misidentified as a marquee.
- WCAG 2.2 AA; 44px tap floor; focus ring 2px ink.

## Patterns

- **Card**: square photo, glass badge for when ("Tonight · 19:00", "On now · till 23:00"), heart to save, title (two lines), venue · area, walk · price · why tag. No photo: the kind's pictogram on its tint.
- **Picture identity**: an event shows its own artwork; without it, its venue's logo or photo (marked "Venue logo" or "Venue photo" on the detail page); without either, its category pictogram. A venue's logo (`image_source: logo`) is drawn whole on a white tile wherever a venue photo would sit: place rows, map cards, the venue link on an event, saved-list tiles, and, with a credit, the venue detail. Photo retrieval and review rules are in `docs/data.md`.
- **Row** (Programme, venue programme, Saved): rail with time then walk (area when location is off), kind and why tag, English title, venue · area · price, thumbnail. A dated listing without a supplied time says "Time not listed", never claims to run all day.
- **Place row**: photo or pictogram, name, kind · area, open state (filled dot open, square shut, dashed not filed), walk.
- **Event page**: photo or pictogram, English title, 1–2 sentence English highlights, explicit performance languages when stated, facts as an icon list (When, Entry, Walk or Area), the ticket bar (price and the ticket seller's host, Tickets or Walk me there), Calendar / Walk there / Save / List, the Going row, the venue card, a native Original description disclosure, Address (mini-map, address, Walking directions), provenance. On phones the tab bar steps aside and a back key appears.
- **Flags**: what the source says about a show, from `flag`. Cancelled and postponed are solid ink pills; the card or row greys its photo, strikes the title, never reads "On now", and sorts last on Tonight's shelves; the event page opens with an ink notice and drops tickets, calendar and Going. Sold out and few tickets left are outlined pills; sold out turns Tickets into "Check for returns". Never the accent, which means now.
- **Going** (`going.js`): "I'm going" marks the event in this browser (`wa:going:v1`) and, when signed in, in `going`. The row shows the public count from `going_counts` ("12 going", "You and 11 others"); nobody sees who. Signed out, the mark stays local and a toast offers sign-in. If the count can't be read, the row shows no number.
- **Original description**: closed by default. Opening it fetches at most 2,000 source characters, the original title and a link to the full source. Failed requests offer Try again and retain the source link. Known text languages use `lang`; performance languages come from explicit source statements, never from the announcement's language. English-source excerpts are labelled Source description. English highlights also feed page metadata and social previews.
- **Mini-map**: a still MapLibre map with the pin, in the page's theme; tapping it opens `map.html?pick=<id>`, which centres on that event or place and chooses it.
- **Venue page**: photo or a monogram of the name on its kind's tint, open state, Follow / Walk there / Save, labelled links (Website, Instagram, Facebook), a Next fact (the next show and how many follow), programme by day, the week's hours, Address with the mini-map, provenance. Retained closed venues show a closure notice and never read Open now from old hours.
- **Follows** (`follow.js`) are keyed by identity: `place:<places.id>` for a venue, `src:<handle>` for a source. The stored value is the label You shows. Follows saved as bare names migrate to ids when the catalogue loads (a name shared by two places stays bare). A followed venue or source gets a Calendar link (webcal, the `calendar-feed` function with `place` or `handle`); the calendar app does the notifying, nothing is sent from the site. The source page has its own Follow button.
- **Email alerts** (You, signed in only): two switches, both off until turned on: weekly digest and changes to events you saved or marked going. Follows also sync to the account (`follows`), like saves and going. Sending is described in `docs/data.md`.
- **Flag a problem** (`report.js`): a disclosure under "Where this came from" on an event page. One of five fixed reasons and an optional note of at most 280 characters, inserted into `problem_reports` (insert-only). `/review` lists the open ones with Fixed and Dismiss.
- **Measure from** (Programme filters): a place we hold, picked from a list, saved as `wa:anchor:v1`. It stands in for the device position in `WA.Geo` (`anchor`, `setAnchor`), so walking times and the distance slider work with no location prompt. Clearing the box returns to the device position.
- **Tab bar** (phones): a vermilion-tinted pill marks the current tab. (`WA.glassDrop`, in `tabbar.js`, is the one implementation; the Map's Show control uses it too.) Press the bar (hold or start sliding) and it swells slightly while a drop of clear glass, taller and wider than a tab, lifts under the finger. The drop carries a magnified vermilion copy of the tabs clipped to its shape, so a tab half under it is half tinted. Letting go settles the drop on the nearest tab and opens it; a quick tap just follows the link.
- **Search field** (Tonight, phones): a real `type=search` input in the pill, with a vermilion submit key. Enter sends the words to `discover.html?q=`, where the ask field reads a sentence as well as a title; empty goes to the Programme with its field focused. From 1024 the pill is Where / When / What slots that open the finder sheet.
- **Keys under the headline** (Tonight): Near me and Pick your kinds, pills in the accent's tint with a 20px line icon (`nav`, `kinds`), so the two features are seen. The row keeps its height in every state. Near me asks for the location on the tap (nothing prompts on load), then Tonight opens with a "Near you tonight" shelf (else the next listed day): what is on, closest on foot first, within 30 minutes' walk when anything is, else the six closest with a line saying so. The key goes away once the location is known. A denial leaves "Location is off" and a line on where to allow it. Walking times print bold with the walk icon on cards, rows and place rows. Other pages keep `R.locPrompt`.
- **Rows that scroll sideways** (the kind row on Tonight, the chip rows on Programme and Places): the edge with more behind it fades out over 44px (30px on the kind row, whose half-visible tile must stay legible), and the fade follows the scroll (right at the start, both edges in the middle, left at the end; none when the row fits). `ui-helpers.js` sets `is-more-start` and `is-more-end` from the scroll position and size; `wa.css` draws the fade with a mask on `--fade-l` and `--fade-r`. A cut-off item reads as "more this way" without an arrow or a gradient overlay, so it works on any background, in both themes.
- **Light / Dark button** (top bar, every page, left of You): a bare 42px line icon with no disc, showing the mode a tap switches to (the sun while dark, the moon while light). One tap sets an explicit Light or Dark (`WA.Theme.set`, built in `theme.js` so no page repeats the markup); Auto stays on You under Appearance. Its label names the same mode.
- **Kind row** (Tonight): the first kind that does not fit is always left about half in view (`peek()` in `home.js` sets `--cat-gap`, 2–24px, from the measured tile widths, so it adapts to any screen width and text size rather than to device models; it moves to the neighbouring item when the gap would leave that range, and re-runs when tile sizes change; on a phone the tiles are 56px wide so five kinds fit fully and the sixth peeks, and both rows snap to item starts with `scroll-snap-type: x proximity`; from 1024 the whole row fits). Pictograms on their own tinted tiles; the chosen kind gets an ink ring, not a tab underline. The sticky part is the search pill alone; the kind row sits below it in the flow and scrolls away with the page. Nothing changes height on scroll (a folding sticky header shrank, scroll anchoring pulled `scrollY` back under the threshold and the bar flickered), so keep it that way: `is-scrolled` only draws the pill bar's hairline.
- **Map**: one row of controls, Show (All · Events · Places) and When, both 44px glass. Show is the tab bar's slider in small: a vermilion-tinted pill under the current option, and pressing the capsule lifts the glass drop, which picks the option it settles on; a tap still just presses. Events are pills with the start time (vermilion when on now); places are round pins with their pictogram, ringed when open now. The places layer is what is open now plus the rooms hosting an event in the chosen window. Pins cluster in screen space; the chosen pin never clusters. Things at one address (within 12 m: a venue and the show in it, two shows in one hall) cannot be split by zooming, so from zoom 16 groups of two to six fan out around their shared point, each pin its own and tappable; a bigger group (a cinema's week) stays a count, and tapping it zooms to street level, then opens the list sheet. Choosing a pin raises a row of cards for what is in view (in the sheet's order); swiping the row moves the choice from pin to pin, and the map slides only when that pin is hidden. Tapping bare map closes the cards. On phones the sheet drags between peek, half and full (a flick goes one stop on); the list drags it too when it cannot scroll that way itself. The locate key rides above the sheet and hides when the sheet is full or cards are up. `?show=events|places` keeps the Show choice; `?pick=<id>` opens on one event or place.
- **Map style**: `map-style.json` (day) and `map-style-dusk.json` (night) are generated by `npm run build:map-styles` from one layer list and two palettes in `.scripts/build-map-styles.js`; edit the palette there, not the JSON. Tiles and fonts are OpenFreeMap. Colour carries the city (green parks, blue water, warm main roads, dashed footpaths); there are no POI icons, because our pins are the points of interest.
- **Programme and search**: one pill field for a title, a venue or a sentence, with the filters key inside it; one scrolling row of quick chips (what a sentence set, removable, then Tonight · Tomorrow · Weekend · This week, a hairline, then the kinds with their pictograms, no counts). A chosen When chip, tapped again, clears it. Filters holds When (Tonight, Tomorrow, This weekend, This week, and Pick dates, which opens native From and To date fields; one date is a day, two are a range, `?date=&to=`), area, order, start time, distance and switches. A focused empty field offers four example questions. A title or a venue is searched as typed. A sentence ("free jazz tonight in Kalamaja", "джаз сегодня", "täna tasuta kontsert") is read in the page by `ask.js` into those same filters plus the words left to look for, and a line under the chips says what it understood, with "Search the words" to undo. Only when words are left that the page could not place and nothing matches is `/api/ask` asked (see `docs/models.md`); its answer also only sets filters, and it is dropped if it finds nothing. Locally understood date, kind, free, language and price constraints remain authoritative. Changing a query/filter or choosing "Search the words" invalidates pending model responses; weekday names in English, Estonian and Russian are read locally. Model place words remain mandatory. The list is always our own listings.
- **Never empty**: Tonight falls back to the next listed day. Programme offers one compact recovery action that brings most listings back while retaining other filters. Query text stays in the field, never in a button label; reset is available in Filters. When no single change helps, the empty state offers “Start over”.
- **Mobile density**: On now artwork is capped at 162.5px below 768px; its time, title and venue sit below, with a short On now badge. Other shelves keep their normal size. The map has 12px side gutters (16px around the desktop map). Below 1024px, the centred preview is about 70% of the former width, with neighbours at 86% of that size. Tapping a neighbour centres it; tapping the active card opens it. Swiping or focusing a card moves the selected pin. Save and close retain 44px targets on the active card; neighbours expose just the card link. Reduced motion disables the scale transition.
- **Detail artwork**: from 600 to 1023px the media occupies 65% of the content width. At desktop sizes its column is 65% of its former width, leaving more room for the event facts. Phone artwork remains full width. Poster links have their own flex boxes, click areas and keyboard focus outlines.
- **Address previews**: mount MapLibre when the section comes into view, resize with the container and show a loading message until the basemap is ready. Failed bundle, WebGL or tile loading offers an explicit Open map link instead of a blank pin. The complete map remains available from the preview.
- **Venue recommendations**: Places, Tonight venue suggestions and venue pins request only fresh verified records from the `venues` view. Retained direct details for an unverified venue say “Status unverified”; a closed venue says “Listed as closed”. Neither gets an Open now claim from old hours. Verification and review rules are in `docs/data.md`.
- Venue programmes and the map's time order compare the date before the clock, so an early screening next week cannot become “Next” ahead of tonight.
- Areas print in the names visitors use (`R.area`: Põhja-Tallinna → Põhja-Tallinn, All-linn → Old Town, Kesklinna → City centre).
- Interests (`wa:interests:v1`) give Tonight a "For you" shelf and nothing else. There is no card on the page: the key under Tonight's headline ("Pick your kinds", then "Your kinds · 2") opens a sheet where each tap saves at once and the shelf follows; You keeps the same chips. Up to three; the rest disable. The last visit (`wa:visit:v1`) powers "N new since Thursday".
- Location is asked for only on a tap; when already granted, walking times fill in by themselves.

`wa:anchor:v1`, `wa:appearance`, `wa:city`, `wa:going:v1`, `wa:seen:v1`, `wa:follows`, `wa:lists:v1`, `wa:interests:v1`, `wa:visit:v1`, `wanderalt:bookmarks:v1`, `wanderalt:session:v1`. New keys: `wa:` prefix, `:v1` suffix for structured shapes.

## Working locally

- `npm test` includes fixture-based pipeline tests and browser-script VM regression tests for catalogue identity, saved-state recovery, date handling, service-worker caching and middleware security. `npm run typecheck` checks the pipeline and those tests. Real browser checks remain necessary for layout, focus, maps and deployed Functions.
- `_headers` governs static responses; `functions/_middleware.js` also applies the security policy to Function responses. Keep the two policies aligned. A local `npm start` does not run Functions or enforce the deployment CSP; verify those on a Pages preview.
- `npm start`, open touched pages at 390 and 1440 in both themes (You → Appearance).
- The service worker serves stale files while debugging. Clear it, and bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` when the shell changes:
  ```js
  caches.keys().then(k => Promise.all(k.map(x => caches.delete(x))));
  navigator.serviceWorker.getRegistrations().then(r => r.forEach(x => x.unregister()));
  ```
- MapLibre gives its container `position: relative`; `.map-canvas.maplibregl-map` restores absolute positioning.
- Brand: a route that spells a W and ends under a night spark, white on the vermilion tile, with the lowercase Geologica wordmark. The opening (`brand-reveal.js`, styles under `.wa-splash` in `wa.css`) plays every time the app is opened, on any page a link can land on (event and source pages too; not 404 or review), and not when moving between the site's own pages: a page reached from another page of this site, or by Back and Forward, skips it: the tile pops, the route draws itself, the spark lights, "wander" slides in and "alt" arrives off the line and drops onto it, then a tagline. It holds until the page beneath is ready (catalogue drawn, fonts in, first pictures decoded; at least 1.7 s, at most 3.4 s), then the mark flies to its place in the top bar while the cover lifts. An opening is a load with no same-origin referrer (a shared link, a bookmark, the home-screen icon, a typed address) or a reload; nothing is stored. Reduced motion (including the phone's Reduce Motion setting) and prerendering skip it; without JavaScript there is no cover. The mark lives once, in `.scripts/build-brand.js`, which writes every SVG in `brand/`, the inline top-bar mark in each page and the splash markup; edit it there, then run `npm run build:brand` and `npm run build:icons`.
- Brand icons are SVG masters in `brand/`; `npm run build:icons` rasterises the PNGs and `favicon.ico`. Social cards use outlined Geologica 700 from the bundled font so their PNGs do not depend on installed fonts. Icon, manifest and OG references carry `?v=route-w` (bump it in every page, `manifest.webmanifest` and `functions/_middleware.js` when the mark changes) to refresh the immutable brand assets without changing their paths. `og:image` and `twitter:image` are absolute URLs, because Telegram and other scrapers do not resolve relative ones. `supabase/functions/og-image/brand.ts` is generated with the icons; redeploy the function after a mark change.

## Speed

- **Snapshot.** `supabase.js` keeps the last catalogue in `localStorage` (`wa:catalogue:v2`, with a fingerprint of its text, and `wa:catalogue:at`, the time it was last confirmed; trusted for six hours, the pipeline's cadence, and never when that time is ahead of the clock) and draws it at once on a return visit, with `DATA_LIVE` false. Successful fresh responses confirm it; service-worker fallback responses retain their original cache timestamp and never renew the confirmation or become live. Actual event dates in Tallinn take precedence over snapshot labels such as Tonight.
- **Service worker.** Only exact public catalogue paths on the configured backend with the shipped public anon credentials enter the data cache, including catalogue redirects. Signed-in or secret-key requests bypass it. Only successful responses replace cache entries; an upstream HTTP error or network outage retains the last good catalogue. Static revalidation keeps failed responses out of the shell cache.
- **Intl formatters** in `when.js`, `geo.js`, `hours.js` and `render.js` are built once and their answers remembered. Building one per call cost about a second per filter tap on a few hundred listings; keep it that way.
- **Programme** answers a tap in the same frame with the chips, count and summary; the list and panel follow in the next task and repeated taps are drawn once. Markup is written only when it changed (`put`), so an unchanged list keeps its pictures.
- **CSS.** Rows below the fold use `content-visibility: auto`. Cards and day headings carry no live backdrop blur (dozens per screen); glass stays on the few things that float. `touch-action: manipulation` on controls.
- **Map.** MapLibre and the map start with the page, in parallel with the catalogue; the first fit waits for both. Pins are redrawn at most once a frame while the map moves.
- **Navigation.** `speculation.json` (sent as the `Speculation-Rules` header) prefetches the tab bar pages and any same-origin link on hover or press, and prerenders event pages on hover or press. Pages must be safe to prerender: no side effects at load, which is why the last-visit stamp is Tonight only, `detail.js` marks an event seen on `prerenderingchange`, and `brand-reveal.js` stands down while `document.prerendering`. `_headers` sends Early Hints for the home page (stylesheet, font, Supabase connection). Pages preconnect to Supabase; the map also to the tile host.
