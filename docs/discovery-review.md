# Now and Map interaction review

Reviewed 6 October 2026 against live WanderAlt, live Meetup web search, `docs/design-brief.md`, `docs/frontend.md`, and the current source. This is an expert review and a prototype, not a usability study or production deployment. The Cloudflare preview is checked separately.

## Observations and decisions

| Feedback | Evidence and challenge | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Dates on Now and Map | Now has three shortcuts; Map has a separate select. Map's Tomorrow resets to Today on Now. Travellers staying several days have a real planning need. A permanently visible calendar would cost space and keyboard complexity. | Add a labelled Pick dates action to both. Keep Today, Tomorrow and Weekend/This week as shortcuts. Use native date fields in an on-demand sheet; one day by default, optional inclusive end date. Keep an explicit empty selection instead of silently switching days. |
| Preserve moods, price, Near me and dates | Art & film + €20 produced 9 listings on Now; Map showed 14 located events including €30 and €44 listings. Moods already persist but Map never reads them. Dates live separately in page state. | Share date semantics and the existing mood/price and origin preferences. Show their values on both pages. Explicit URLs override saved dates. Relative shortcuts reset after the Tallinn day changes; absolute future dates remain in this tab, and an active trip range retains its remaining days; taste remains a device preference. Device coordinates keep their existing five-minute session lifetime. Price applies to tickets, not places with no held prices. |
| Simplify search toward Meetup | Mobile Now is already a real field; desktop replaces it with Where/When/What and another sheet. Search for “jazz tomorrow under 20” works without needing a model. Meetup's live search is a direct field with separate filters. | One quiet real field at both widths. Keep Enter, a 44px submit key, and the existing Programme sentence reader. Avoid live model requests or a second search mode. |
| Collapse and drag the route | At 390px the route takes about 400px and Events/Places sits near y=725. But the brief makes the walk the product's unit. An anonymous scroll icon hides that purpose, and a floating draggable object competes with the bottom navigation, keyboard, sheets and event links. | Collapse into a labelled inline “A walk for now” disclosure with a real route title. Keep Walk it, Another and the origin picker when expanded, with reduced-motion-aware motion. Reject floating/repositioning for recommendations. An active route may merit a persistent control later, after observation. |
| Show Events/Places after closing filters; glass treatment | Visibility is mostly an ordering problem, not a missing animation. A second glass layer over photos would violate the system. | Compact route first; reuse the existing glass segment/drop for the switch. Keep it in normal flow and return focus sensibly after sheets. No blur on event content. |
| Date control after Weekend | This repeats the first date request; two home calendar entry points add complexity. Hiding empty shortcuts also makes the controls unstable. | One calendar action after the shortcuts. Keep Today even when empty; show an honest empty result and recovery. |
| Larger event cards / NYT hierarchy | Now deliberately overrides shared rows down to 16px titles, two lines and 56px pictures; three narrow columns crowd long titles. Supabase's upcoming Tallinn snapshot has 865 rows, 593 with event imagery and 379 without price. These raw database counts are not the filtered live feed. Huge poster cards would waste space for the rest. | An image-first single-column feed on Now and Programme: large identified artwork, then kind plus scene label, a strong wrapping title, time/walking facts, venue/area/price and a small source line. The owner supplied Meetup and NYT screenshots during the review and clarified image above text. Known missing art uses a short Label panel; a photo failing after load keeps its reserved size to avoid a layout jump. Keep missing-time/image/price states honest. No uniform poster grid, invented photos, ratings or attendees. |

## Research and limits

- **Meetup, directly inspected 6 Oct 2026:** [Find](https://www.meetup.com/find/?location=ee--Tallinn&source=EVENTS) exposes a text field, location, Events/Groups, Day/size/venue/distance filters, and Day → Custom opens an October calendar. Cards lead with imagery, title, date/time and organizer. This is web evidence, not a native-app audit. [Finding an event](https://help.meetup.com/hc/en-us/articles/39235072484109-Finding-an-event) documents keyword search and calendar filters. [2026 roadmap](https://www.meetup.com/blog/2026-meetup-roadmap/) dates its mobile redesign to 18 Dec 2025. [2025 redesign](https://www.meetup.com/blog/new-design-2025/) describes a product-specific identity; it is not evidence that its stickers or social proof suit WanderAlt.
- **Filter visibility:** Baymard's [applied-filter overview research, updated 13 May 2026](https://baymard.com/research-articles/how-to-design-applied-filters) supports showing and removing choices outside the closed panel. Its [2025 list/filter benchmark](https://baymard.com/research-articles/current-state-product-list-and-filtering) concerns commerce. Applying it to event navigation is an inference; it does not establish an ideal persistence lifetime. Same-task Now/Map continuity is the product decision here.
- **Dates:** [Material date pickers](https://m2.material.io/components/date-pickers) distinguish single-date and range use. [WAI date-picker dialog example](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/examples/datepicker-dialog/) illustrates the substantial keyboard/focus work in custom calendars. Native inputs reduce code and allow platform calendar/typed entry, but physical iOS/Android testing remains necessary. Dates refer to Tallinn, not the visitor's timezone; a run overlapping the selected day should be included.
- **Floating content, including current autumn guidance:** Android's [notification bubbles guidance, updated 1 Oct 2026](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/notifications/bubbles) limits bubbles to important ongoing communication or explicitly requested content and warns they cover other content. Apple's [Picture in Picture](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/multitask-with-picture-in-picture-iphcc3587b5d/ios) is a production movable/collapsible window for ongoing video. These prove the mechanics exist, not that a recommendation needs them. No directly applicable Autumn 2026 study demonstrating a benefit for draggable discovery recommendations was found. [WCAG dragging movements](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html) adds non-drag pointer and keyboard alternatives: repositioning would require more UI, bounds, safe-area and cancellation logic.
- **Progressive disclosure:** [NN/g's principle](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/) is older foundational guidance, still linked by its [18 Dec 2025 Web UX guide](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-ux-study-guide/). A meaningful label and visible first-level answer matter. Making the walk compact is a hypothesis to test for both listing-first and undecided visitors; fewer visible pixels alone do not prove better discovery.
- **Glass:** [Apple WWDC25](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/) reserves glass for navigation/controls above content and warns against glass stacking. [NN/g, 10 Oct 2025](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/liquid-glass/) challenges transparency, target crowding and lost predictability. Reusing our existing surface and gesture code is justified by consistency, not fashion. Keep solid fallbacks, visible selected state, focus and 44px controls.
- **Feed hierarchy:** [NN/g, 14 Nov 2025](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/good-visual-design/) supports consistent grids, typography, intentional color and useful imagery. NYT browser access was blocked by the browser's site-safety policy; no claim is made about its October 2026 native app. The owner supplied screenshots of Meetup and NYT: Meetup has photo above title/facts; NYT has a large headline and media. They are direct visual references, not independently dated app-version evidence. Follow the requested image/text hierarchy with WanderAlt typography and tokens; copying the social proof, black palette or NYT headline face would conflict with this brief.

## iPad and constrained windows

[Apple WWDC25: Elevate the design of your iPad app](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/208/) treats orientation and window resizing as changes in available space and recommends adapting navigation and columns. This is native guidance applied by analogy to our web app, not an instruction to imitate SwiftUI. As of 6 Oct 2026 we cannot cite research from November or December 2026.

Keep our existing breakpoints and navigation rather than detecting an iPad user agent. At 600–1023px the Now column is bounded, with a centred 560px discovery sheet; phones have a bottom sheet. At 1024–1199px the existing top navigation and side-by-side layout remain, with a 260px home map card and 324px Map sidebar instead of consuming 440px. Narrower split windows return to the compact layout. Touch targets, visible actions and keyboard support stay available at every width. Resizing never clears choices. Platform keyboards, date dialogs and real safe areas still need hardware testing.

## Validation plan

At 390×844 and 1440×1000px, plus iPad mini 6 portrait 744×1133 and landscape 1133×744, light and dusk: first-screen switch visibility; route expand/collapse/Another; search Enter; date single/range, cancellation, invalid/past dates, empty day and overlapping runs; mood + submood + cap Now→Map→Now; origin and nearest state in both directions; explicit links, reload/back; keyboard/focus, language redraw, 44px targets, overflow and reduced motion. Compare listing IDs under identical filters (Map excludes unlocated listings). Exercise missing images and provenance; preserve catalogue/offline behavior. Run `npm test` and typecheck before pushing. Physical mobile Safari and native date dialogs remain a separate verification gate.

## Prototype results, 6 October

- `npm test`: 337 passed, zero failures; `npm run typecheck` passed. Inline icon sync changed no pages. Nine discovery and detail behavioural tests exercise page-lifetime storage and back/forward cache, malformed dates, future-trip continuity, overlapping runs and autumn DST, mood/submood/cap semantics, hostile listing fields and image-failure recovery.
- Now and Map were checked in both themes at all four planned sizes. A 320px constrained window also had no horizontal page overflow; its mood sheet body scrolls while actions remain visible. The mini landscape sheet leaves its actions within the 744px viewport. English, Estonian, Russian and Ukrainian redraws were exercised. These are browser viewport checks, not device emulation of Safari or its keyboard.
- Art & film/€20 stayed selected on Map; 8–10 October showed the same range in its date sheet. Selecting Film and Tomorrow on Map returned to Now with four listings, including an explicitly unknown price. Programme opened from Now and Map's located listings matched those four IDs. Map omits unlocated listings and its drawer shows only the current camera's contents, so its in-view count is not a total-feed count.
- A selected Kino Sõprus starting point and nearest intent survived both directions; turning nearest off on Map returned it off on Now. No device-location permission was granted during this pass. Past-date submission remained in the dialog, cancellation discarded drafts, and an empty future day stayed visibly selected with recovery actions.
- At 390px the collapsed walk brings Events/Places to about y=453, versus about y=725 in production. Applying moods with the walk expanded also returned focus to the selected segment and brought it above the tab bar. Enter search, route expansion/collapse, Another focus, date-sheet cancellation and reduced-motion state were checked. Reduced-motion motion timing was not measured.
- Photos are lazy, dimensioned and identified; no dependencies, model calls on typing or additional data endpoint were added. Bigger imagery costs more image bandwidth and vertical scrolling than compact rows; initial counts and Programme pagination remain bounded. This pass did not establish a Lighthouse score, network budget or measured rendering improvement. During rapid automated cross-page navigation the browser emitted one native view-transition abort; the resulting page and filters were correct, with no application exception observed.

The main maintenance cost is a shared date contract across three consumers, including URL precedence, midnight rollover and back/forward restoration. Those behaviours have real fixture tests. Native date fields retain some platform variation but avoid maintaining a custom calendar's keyboard model. The walk disclosure adds one deliberate tap and can reduce its discoverability; a meaningful label and route title mitigate this, and observation is still required. Glass reuses the existing component rather than introducing another effect. The feed keeps facts and provenance, at the cost of fewer events per viewport; condensed Map rows remain useful for spatial scanning. Programme's general keyword/category search remains independent of Map's discovery preferences: its Browse map link transfers supported dates, not the complete search query. Full listing-search continuity is tracked in [DEV-33](https://linear.app/pm-career-transition/issue/DEV-33/wa-carry-programme-listing-searches-into-map).

## Additional audit

- Reproduced Now Tomorrow → Map Today → browser Back restoring the stale Tomorrow URL during a cold history load. History now uses the latest shared selection and rewrites the returned URL; Back followed by reload stays Today. A normal new explicit link still overrides dates. Programme no longer changes discovery preferences merely by reading its own URL. Tests cover both cold history and back/forward cache, blocked storage through midnight, and first visits without stored context. [MDN navigation type](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/PerformanceNavigationTiming/type) distinguishes history traversal from an ordinary navigation or reload.
- `?date=2027-02-30` previously became a misleading March heading with an empty date field. Programme now validates real calendar keys. Chosen dates show month and future year. Calendar links and same-day feed headings were checked; all-day feeds say Today, while compact evening headings retain Tonight.
- The new feed title is protected from interface lookup, venue names have explicit literal markers, and Save/Saved/Remove labels use raw title placeholders. Map count/date summaries are separate translation pieces; Picked translates while its venue name stays literal. English, Estonian, Russian and Ukrainian fixtures protect a source title named Festival. Russian Map was checked with a long 2027 range and a real long venue name.
- At 320px, forced single-row filters crowded Russian words. Mood, origin and date keys now wrap within the screen and retain at least 44px targets. Studio Gallery K28 - Kentmanni galerii and a November–December 2027 range fit Now and Map. Current Now/Map were rechecked at 390, 744, 1133 and 1440px in both themes; Programme and Saved at 390/1440px in both themes. No horizontal page overflow was found. Feed radius and walk easing now use the existing tokens. Rapid keyboard expansion/collapse ends in the right state without losing focus; animation timing was not measured.
- Saved had nested Remove buttons inside event/place links. They are now siblings, with unchanged placement and measured 44×44px targets. Keyboard removal and immediate Undo restored the saved item. Detail Save previously rebuilt the page, closed the open source excerpt and lost focus. It now updates in place; source loading, Save and Undo were checked in the browser, with a fixture for canonical aliases and restoration of list memberships.
- A read-only catalogue check found date-only/timed candidates for American-ish and Enigma, including conflicting venue information. These may be duplicate announcements, but identity is not established by matching titles alone. No rows were hidden or merged. Source comparison and a safe review path are tracked in [DEV-32](https://linear.app/pm-career-transition/issue/DEV-32/wa-review-date-only-and-timed-copies-of-the-same-listing).

Physical Safari, location permission/recovery and listing-first versus undecided visitor tasks are tracked in [DEV-31](https://linear.app/pm-career-transition/issue/DEV-31/wa-validate-discovery-on-iphone-and-ipad-mini). No production deployment was performed.
